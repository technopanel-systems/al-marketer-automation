Special cases

Website Building · بناء الموقعOne build: weeks 3–7; dashboard week 8; optimisation report week 12. In P2: build weeks 6–10, dashboard week 11, optimisation week 12.
SEO · الظهور في البحث (SEO)First optimisation report in week 12 only.
Media Production · الإنتاج الفنيFinal Media Asset Batch in week 3, before content or ads that use it.
Influencer Marketing · تسويق المؤثرينStarts in P2 by default; execution no earlier than week 6; performance report at least 2 weeks after execution.
Marketing Automation · أتمتة التسويقImplemented workflows no earlier than week 4, and after email execution when Email Marketing is also selected.
Performance Marketing · تسويق الأداءCampaign execution waits until client readiness confirms tracking is installed and ad-account access is granted.

Hard rules

  • Week 1 contains only the 3 strategic deliverables, in order (Blueprint rule 08).
  • Preparation is compressed; execution takes most of the time (rule 09).
  • Monitoring and optimisation only after execution exists (rule 10).
  • Every dependency is respected; a schedule that breaks one is rejected.
  • Nothing is silently cut: anything that does not fit in 12 weeks is listed as 'continues after month 3' and flagged at Gate 2.
