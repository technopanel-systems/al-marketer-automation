AL-MARKETER CONTROL CENTER - START HERE
=======================================

The system turns a client's name, website and meeting notes into a designed Arabic technical proposal.
Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is sent to clients by the system.

WHAT YOU NEED (check before you start)
  [ ] A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer (8 GB memory recommended, 4 GB minimum).
  [ ] About 2 GB of free disk space.
  [ ] Internet.
  [ ] Your OWN Claude account with a paid plan: Pro works for testing, Max is better for daily use.
      (The free Claude plan cannot run it. Check or upgrade at https://claude.ai/upgrade)
  [ ] Your Claude email and password at hand (you sign in once, in the browser).
  Nothing else: the setup brings Node.js, the browser and Claude Code by itself. No admin password needed.

SETUP (once, about 10 minutes)
  1. Save the zip file (for example in Downloads).
  2. Right-click the zip > Properties > at the bottom tick "Unblock" (if you see it) > OK.
  3. Right-click the zip > "Extract All..." > change the folder to  C:\  > Extract.
     This makes the folder C:\Al-Marketer. (Do not run anything from inside the zip, and do not use
     a OneDrive or Desktop folder.)
  4. Open the folder C:\Al-Marketer and double-click  SETUP.cmd
     - If a blue window says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" > "Run anyway".
  5. Watch the window. It goes through 7 steps and says OK for each one:
       1 checks the computer, 2 Node.js, 3 app components and a browser (downloads ~300 MB),
       4 Claude Code (official installer), 5 sign in, 6 final check, 7 Desktop shortcut.
     At step 5 a browser opens: sign in with your Claude account and click "Authorize",
     then go back to the setup window.
  6. At the end it says "Setup finished" and opens the Control Center in your browser.
     A shortcut "Al-Marketer Control Center" is now on your Desktop.
  If a line says [FAIL]: do what the arrow (->) under it says, then double-click SETUP.cmd again.
  Running SETUP.cmd again is always safe: it only does what is missing.

EVERY DAY
  - Double-click "Al-Marketer Control Center" on the Desktop. The app opens in your browser
    (http://localhost:4317). Keep the black window open while you work; close it to stop.
  - Left menu > "How to use" explains every page.
  - Once a month (optional): double-click  UPDATE-CLAUDE.cmd  in the Al-Marketer folder.
    It updates the Claude program and shows which model does each job. The system always asks for the
    newest Opus / Sonnet / Haiku by itself, so there is nothing to choose or change.

A FIRST TEST (about 30 minutes, uses your Claude plan)
  1. "New proposal": a company name, its website (for example example.com), market, industry,
     a few lines of notes. Click "Create and start research".
  2. Research: for each suggested competitor choose "Compare" or "Not a competitor", "Save decisions".
  3. Wait; the page updates by itself. Answer questions only if some appear.
  4. Diagnosis: confirm the problems, "Approve diagnosis". Scope & plan: "Approve scope".
  5. Proposal: the slides appear. Try "Ask the AI to change something" with a small change.
     Tick the fact-check box, "Approve proposal".
  6. Delivery: download the PDF; the internal report follows a few minutes later.
  Finished testing? In the Proposals list, "..." on the row > "Delete permanently".
  (A FULL COPY also holds the proposals already made; open them from the Proposals list.)

OPTIONAL FREE KEYS (left menu > Settings & keys; everything works without them)
  If you received a FULL COPY, the keys are already saved: see PACKAGE-INFO.txt. Nothing to do.
  - Google PageSpeed: website speed and SEO scores (recommended).
  - SerpApi: Google results when Google asks the browser to verify (250 free searches a month).
  - YouTube, Apify: extra social media numbers.
  Each key has "How to get it" steps on that page.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - "Claude could not run" or "not signed in": open PowerShell, type  claude auth login  and sign in,
    then click "Try again" in the app.
  - "You have hit your usage limit": your Claude plan's limit; wait for it to reset, then "Try again".
  - A step failed: it shows "Try again" on top of the page. Only that step runs again.
  - The page does not open: is the black window open? If not, use the Desktop shortcut.
  - Anything else: send a screenshot and the file  setup-report.txt  (in the Al-Marketer folder).

PRIVACY
  The proposals you make stay in the "clients" folder on your computer. The AI runs through your own
  Claude account. Website and social checks read public pages without logging in anywhere.
  Because client information goes through Claude, turn OFF "Help improve Claude" once at
  https://claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls
